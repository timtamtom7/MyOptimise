# The New Optimise Workflow: A Guide to the Future

**Goal:** Transform "Optimise" from a standard agency management tool into a specialized manufacturing line for high-quality social media content. This shift moves away from "general employees" doing everything to a specialized **Strategist + Editor** model.

---

## 1. The Big Picture: Why We Are Changing
Currently, employees do everything: strategy, design, video editing, and posting. This leads to burnout and lower quality because one person rarely excels at *everything*.

**The New Model:**
We are splitting the work into two specialized roles:
1.  **The Architect (Strategist):** The brain. They do the research, come up with the ideas, and write the "blueprints" (briefs).
2.  **The Builder (Editor):** The hands. They take the blueprint and build the final product (video/graphic) perfectly.

This allows us to scale faster. We can hire many freelance Editors (paid per video) while keeping a smaller core team of expert Strategists.

---

## 2. The Four Roles

### 🏛️ 1. The Admin (The Founders)
**Who they are:** The business owners.
**Their Goal:** Oversee the entire machine without getting stuck in the gears.
**What they see:**
*   **The Command Center:** A high-level view of all clients. Who is happy? Who is stuck?
    *   **Client Workload Monitor:** A live grid of every client, showing how many active tasks they have, how many are high-priority, and how many are overdue. This runs directly off the internal task system, so bottlenecks are always visible.
*   **Financial Control:** Automated tracking of "Money In" (client invoices) vs. "Money Out" (editor payouts). The platform calculates, per editor, how much has been earned from approved, priced jobs so Admins can run a clean pay-per-deliverable model.
*   **Quality Control:** The final judge. They can spot-check any strategy or video.
*   **No More Busy Work:** Invoicing and monthly reporting are automated by the system.

### 🧠 2. The Strategist (Manager)
**Who they are:** The creative directors and planners.
**Their Goal:** Create winning strategies and clear instructions.
**What they do:**
*   **Deep Research:** They use the platform's AI tools to find trending audio, hooks, and content pillars for their niche.
*   **The "Brief" Creator:** They don't open video editing software. Instead, they fill out a structured "Brief" in the system:
    *   *Hook:* "Stop doing this mistake..."
    *   *Script:* [Full text]
    *   *Visual Direction:* "Use fast cuts and upbeat music."
    *   *Assets:* [Uploads raw footage link]
*   **The Reviewer:** When the Editor finishes a video, the Strategist checks it against the Brief. If it's good, they approve it for the Client.

### 🎨 3. The Editor (Contractor)
**Who they are:** Freelance video editors or graphic designers.
**Their Goal:** Execute the Brief perfectly and get paid.
**What they see:**
*   **The Job Board:** A simple dashboard with two main lists: "Open Jobs" (what they should be working on right now) and "Completed Work" (what has already been approved).
*   **Clear Instructions:** No guessing. They open a job and see exactly what to build (Script, Assets, Style).
*   **Upload & Done:** They upload the finished file directly into the job. Once the Strategist and Client have approved it, the system marks that job as "Approved" and includes it automatically in their earnings.
*   **My Earnings:** A lightweight earnings view that shows how much they have earned from approved jobs in this workspace, how many jobs they have completed, and how much has been approved this month.
*   **Focus:** They see ZERO strategy, ZERO client billing, and ZERO sensitive business data. Just their work and their earnings.

### 🤝 4. The Client
**Who they are:** The business paying for the service.
**Their Goal:** To see results and approve content easily.
**What they see:**
*   **The "Quiet" Dashboard:** A clean, professional view.
*   **Approval Queue:** They get a notification: "3 New Posts for Review." They watch, click "Approve" or "Request Edit."
*   **Magic Links:** For busy clients, we generate a secure public link. They can click, review, and approve content from their phone *without logging in*.
*   **Results:** A live view of their growth (followers, reach) without needing to ask "how are we doing?"
*   **Service Requests:** If they want an extra email campaign, they click a button to request it officially, rather than sending a WhatsApp message.

## 3. The Workflow: From Idea to Post

1.  **Strategy Phase:** The **Strategist** uses AI to research a topic and creates a **Brief**. They assign it to the pool of Editors.
2.  **Production Phase:** An **Editor** picks up the Brief, downloads the assets, creates the video, and uploads the draft.
3.  **Internal Review:** The **Strategist** watches the draft.
    *   *Bad?* They send it back to the Editor with notes.
    *   *Good?* They approve it for the Client.

72→    *   *Approved?* It goes to the scheduling queue.
73→5.  **Completion:** The post goes live. The **Admin** sees the analytics automatically flow into the monthly report.

---

## 4. The Goal: "The Optimise Standard"
By forcing this workflow, we ensure:
*   **Consistency:** Every video starts with a Strategy.
*   **Speed:** Editors can just edit; they don't need to think about "what to post."
*   **Scalability:** We can add 100 Editors tomorrow without breaking the system.
*   **Professionalism:** Clients stop texting personal numbers and start trusting the Platform.
